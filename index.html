📱 App info
RangerLog v1.4 — built for ranger teams managing green spaces across Torbay and beyond. Log wildlife, hazards, invasive species, infrastructure and path management data — with photo capture, GPS and OS grid references.

🔒 Legal & privacy
What data does RangerLog collect?
When you use RangerLog, the following information may be stored on your device and synced to the team Google Sheet:
📍
GPS location
Used to place your log entries accurately on the map and generate an OS grid reference.
📷
Photos
Stored on your device only. Not synced to Google Sheets.
📝
Field observations
Wildlife, hazards, paths and infrastructure records. Stored locally and synced to the team Google Sheet.
👤
Your name
Used on notice board posts. Visible to other team members in the app.
Where is it stored?
All data is stored on your device. When the app syncs, entries and path records are sent to a private Google Sheet managed by the team lead. Photos are never synced — they stay on your device only.
Who can see it?
Your ranger team and team lead only. Your data is never shared publicly or sold to third parties. RangerLog contains no adverts and no tracking.
Your rights (UK GDPR)
You have the right to see, correct or request deletion of any data held about you. Speak to your team lead or the app developer. You can also contact the ICO at ico.org.uk if you have a concern.
Data controller: Neil Driver — RangerLog developer.
A full Privacy Notice is available from your team lead.
